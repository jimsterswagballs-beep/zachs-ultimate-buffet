Zach’s Ultimate Buffet — v40 Patch Notes

MASON BORG MELEE CHANGE
- Mason Borg’s regular three-hit melee combo no longer removes enemy bullets.
- His melee swings still deal damage to bosses and retain their combo/finisher effects.
- Bullet reflection remains exclusive to Aegis Counter.
- Separate active abilities that clear projectiles, such as Seismic Slam, are unchanged.

INSTALLATION
Replace both index.html and game.html in the existing GitHub Pages repository with these v40 files. Keep cartman.png and the music folder next to index.html, commit the changes, then hard-refresh both devices after Pages deploys.

VALIDATION
JavaScript syntax and archive integrity were checked. A live multiplayer session was not available for testing.

Zach's Ultimate Buffet v39 — Max & Mason Ability Update

NEW FIGHTER: MAX
- Added Max, a sunglasses-wearing aura-based fighter with a custom cyan/purple sprite.
- Passive Aura: pulses damage to nearby bosses and clears nearby bullets; Aura Overload increases radius and damage for 8 seconds.
- Sunglass Laser: a piercing, aim-directed beam with custom cyan/purple/white effects and boss damage.
- Aura Burst: an area burst that damages a nearby boss and clears projectiles.
- Added Max to solo character selection and the multiplayer lobby; number keys 1–6 and arrow keys now cover all six fighters.

MASON BORG
- Normal 3-hit melee combo no longer reflects bullets back at enemies; it destroys nearby bullets instead.
- Aegis Counter is the dedicated timed reflection ability. Removed the brief reflection effect from Titan Breaker.
- Multiplayer guests use a per-player reflection timer for Aegis Counter rather than activating the host's global reflection timer.

MULTIPLAYER
- Host simulation processes Max's passive aura for remote Max players, so aura damage is authoritative and shared.
- Max's laser and burst effects live in shared game state, so effects can be shown on connected devices.
- Multiplayer character cards now fit all six fighters.

Validation: JavaScript syntax and ZIP structure checked. A live two-device multiplayer session was not available in this environment, so please test character selection and Max's abilities after publishing.
